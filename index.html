Our Mission

Lessening the burdens of government, one project at a time.

Madrone was formed to assist local governments, public agencies, schools, community colleges, colleges and universities, and hospitals in acquiring, developing, and constructing the facilities their communities need — educational, housing, healthcare, and community infrastructure. As 501(c)(3) nonprofit owners, we align every project with the public interest, not private profit.

Student Housing

Modern, affordable on-campus residences delivered through public-private partnerships — with no university debt, backstop, or guarantee required.

Workforce Housing

Attainable housing that lets teachers, nurses, first responders, and essential workers live in the communities they serve.

Healthcare Infrastructure

Through the Madrone Health Foundation, we support hospitals and health systems with facilities and energy-as-a-service solutions.

Community Facilities

Hospitality, civic, and infrastructure projects that anchor local economies — plus support for parks, forests, and urban greening goals.

The Madrone Model

Essential facilities. No burden on our partners.

Our nonprofit ownership model lets institutions deliver critical projects while preserving their debt capacity, credit ratings, and balance sheets.

01

Partner

An institution identifies a facility need. Madrone forms a single-purpose nonprofit entity and enters a long-term ground lease — the institution keeps ownership of its land.

02

Finance

Madrone accesses the tax-exempt bond market through a governmental conduit issuer. The bonds are secured by project revenues — not by the institution's credit or guarantees.

03

Build

Best-in-class developers, builders, and architects deliver the project under Madrone's nonprofit ownership and oversight.

04

Benefit

The facility serves students, workers, and patients for decades. Surplus revenues and, ultimately, the facility itself benefit the partner institution and its community.

Two Foundations, One Mission

The Madrone family of organizations

501(c)(3) Nonprofit

Madrone Community Development Foundation

A California nonprofit public benefit corporation that assists local governments, public agencies, schools, community colleges, colleges and universities in acquiring, developing, and constructing educational, community, infrastructure, and housing facilities.

Madrone CDF is the nonprofit owner behind our student and workforce housing projects nationwide.

501(c)(3) Nonprofit

Madrone Health Foundation

A California nonprofit public benefit corporation dedicated to supporting hospitals and healthcare organizations — helping health systems acquire, develop, and modernize the facilities and infrastructure they need to serve their patients.

Madrone Health leads our healthcare facility and energy-as-a-service initiatives.
