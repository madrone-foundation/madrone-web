University of Memphis

A 542-bed residential community on the Park Avenue Campus, bringing modern apartment-style living to University of Memphis students.

New housing for the Park Avenue Campus

The University of Memphis partnered with Madrone Community Development Foundation to deliver a significant addition to its student housing inventory: a four-story residential community with approximately 542 beds across 242 rental units, located on the university's Park Avenue Campus.

Developed by The Annex Group, the community features studio, two-, and four-bedroom units alongside study and social spaces, outdoor living areas, and grab-and-go dining — while the university preserves its balance sheet for its core academic mission. Opening is scheduled for Fall 2026.

The structure

The project was financed through the sale of approximately $70.9 million in tax-exempt bonds and $2.2 million in federally taxable bonds issued by The Health, Educational and Housing Facility Board of the City of Memphis. Proceeds were loaned to a single-purpose Madrone subsidiary, which owns the project as a 501(c)(3)-controlled entity. Debt service is paid from project revenues — not from university funds, and without any university guarantee.

Highlights
