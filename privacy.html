Privacy Policy

Your privacy, plainly stated.

Effective September 1, 2026 · Applies to madrone.org, the website of Madrone Community Development Foundation and Madrone Health Foundation.

What this site collects

This website is informational. It does not use cookies and does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The only personal information the site collects is what you choose to submit through our contact form.

Like nearly all websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs — such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested — for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used to identify individual visitors.

Third-party services

This site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or embedded content. All resources — including typefaces — are served directly from madrone.org.

Some pages link to external websites — including partner institutions, news coverage, LinkedIn, and the Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board's EMMA system. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Contact form

If you reach out through our contact form, the information you provide — such as your name, organization, email address, and message — is delivered to our team by our hosting provider and used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change — for example, if we add analytics or begin collecting information beyond what our contact form gathers — we will update this policy and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to us through our contact form or by mail to Madrone Community Development Foundation, 2081 Center Street, Berkeley, CA 94704.
