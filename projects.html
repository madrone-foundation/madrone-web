In Development

The pipeline

Beyond our closed financings, Madrone is actively developing projects across four sectors. Details will be announced as each financing closes.

Workforce Housing

Multiple attainable-housing developments in progress with city and public-agency partners, designed for the essential workers communities rely on.

Student Housing

Additional on-campus residential communities in development with university partners, following the same proven ground-lease P3 model.

Hospitality

A hotel development in progress — bringing the nonprofit ownership model to hospitality infrastructure that anchors local economic activity.

Hospital Energy-as-a-Service

Through the Madrone Health Foundation, an energy-as-a-service partnership in development to modernize hospital energy infrastructure with no upfront capital cost to the health system.
