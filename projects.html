In Development

The pipeline

Beyond our closed financings, Madrone is actively developing projects across multiple sectors. Details will be announced as each financing closes.

Workforce Housing

Attainable-housing developments with city and public-agency partners — homes for the teachers, nurses, and first responders communities rely on.

Student Housing

Additional on-campus residential communities with university partners, following the same proven ground-lease partnership model.

Hospitality

A hotel development bringing the nonprofit ownership model to hospitality infrastructure that anchors local economic activity.

Energy & Infrastructure

Grid-scale solar and data center infrastructure for public partners — including utility-scale energy delivered under municipal power purchase agreements.

Hospital Energy-as-a-Service

Additional energy-as-a-service partnerships modernizing hospital energy infrastructure with no upfront capital cost to health systems.
