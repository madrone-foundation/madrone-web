Leadership

Experienced hands. Mission-driven hearts.

Madrone's leadership brings deep experience in public-private partnerships, tax-exempt finance, and complex facility development — in service of a charitable mission.

Our Team

The people behind the projects

Nicholas Waugh

President

Nicholas leads Madrone's mission and strategy, guiding the foundations' partnerships with universities, local governments, and health systems and overseeing each project from concept through financial close and long-term stewardship.

Nicholas came to Madrone from a career in municipal finance — first as a credit rating analyst and later as an investment banker — working on many financings like Madrone's: tax-exempt, project-based transactions for student housing and other essential community facilities. Having evaluated these structures from the rating desk and executed them at the banking table, Nicholas brings both credit discipline and hands-on transaction experience to every Madrone partnership.

Blair Tavenner

Chief Operating Officer

Blair directs Madrone's operations and project execution — coordinating development partners, financing teams, and institutional stakeholders to keep every project on schedule and true to its charitable purpose.

Before joining Madrone, Blair spent a career in real estate development, leading projects of similar scale and complexity from site planning and entitlement through design, construction, and stabilized operations. That developer's perspective — knowing firsthand what it takes to deliver a building on time and on budget — shapes how Madrone selects its partners, structures its projects, and stewards its facilities for the long term.
