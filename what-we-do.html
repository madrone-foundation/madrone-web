What We Do

Nonprofit ownership. Tax-exempt financing. Public benefit.

Madrone serves as the nonprofit owner and borrower for essential facilities, unlocking the tax-exempt bond market for projects that would otherwise strain our partners' balance sheets.

Our Sectors

Facilities that communities depend on

Student Housing

Our core practice. We partner with public and private universities to deliver modern, on-campus residential communities through ground-lease P3 structures. The university keeps its land, its debt capacity, and its credit rating — and gains housing its students can afford.

Workforce Housing

Communities can't function when the people who keep them running can't afford to live there. Madrone works with cities, counties, and public agencies to develop attainable housing for teachers, healthcare workers, first responders, and other essential employees. Multiple workforce housing projects are in active development.

Healthcare & Senior Living

Through the Madrone Health Foundation, we lessen the administrative and financial burdens of hospitals and health systems — developing, owning, and financing healthcare facilities and senior living communities, and delivering energy-as-a-service (EaaS) partnerships that modernize central plants and energy infrastructure with no upfront capital cost to the hospital. A hospital EaaS project is currently in development.

Hospitality & Community Facilities

From conference hotels that anchor campus districts and downtown economies to civic infrastructure, Madrone brings the same nonprofit ownership model to facilities that generate lasting community and economic benefit. A hotel project is currently in our pipeline.

The Madrone Model

How a Madrone partnership works

A proven structure used across billions of dollars of essential facilities nationwide — tailored to each institution's goals.

01

Ground Lease

The institution retains ownership of its land and enters a long-term ground lease with a single-purpose Madrone nonprofit entity created solely for the project.

02

Tax-Exempt Bonds

A governmental conduit issuer sells tax-exempt (and, where needed, taxable) revenue bonds. Proceeds are loaned to the Madrone entity to fund construction. Debt is secured by project revenues alone.

03

Off Balance Sheet

The institution does not finance, backstop, or guarantee the project. Its debt capacity and credit rating are preserved for its core academic, civic, or clinical mission.

04

Professional Delivery

Madrone engages experienced developers, national contractors, and award-winning architects, and retains professional management for operations.

05

Community Benefit

Because the owner is a 501(c)(3), surplus revenues flow back to benefit the project and the partner institution — and at the end of the term, the facility belongs to the institution.
